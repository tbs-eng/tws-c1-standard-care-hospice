Cost & Coverage

Does Medicare cover hospice care?

Yes — hospice care is typically covered by Medicare, Medi-Cal, and most private insurance plans, with no co-pays or deductibles for services related to the hospice diagnosis. For most families, hospice comes at no direct cost.

What's included at no cost to your family

Your care team

Nursing visits, aide support, social worker, chaplain, and volunteer support — coordinated with your own doctor.

Medications

Medications related to the hospice diagnosis — symptom relief and comfort.

Equipment & supplies

Hospital bed, oxygen, wheelchair, and the supplies care requires, delivered to the home.

Coverage, plainly

Medicare

The Medicare hospice benefit covers hospice care for eligible patients — certified by a physician, focused on comfort. Most families pay nothing out of pocket for covered services.

Medi-Cal

Medi-Cal provides hospice coverage for eligible Californians, mirroring the Medicare benefit for those who qualify.

Private insurance

Most plans include a hospice benefit — we help you check yours before care begins.

Questions families ask about cost

The benefit, explained

How the Medicare hospice benefit actually works

Medicare covers hospice under Part A. To qualify, a doctor and the hospice medical director certify a terminal illness with a life expectancy of six months or less if the illness runs its usual course; the patient chooses comfort-focused care instead of treatment meant to cure; and care comes from a Medicare-approved hospice provider.

What it includes

Doctor and nursing services · pain and symptom management · medications related to the terminal illness · medical equipment and supplies · home health aide support · physical, occupational, and speech therapy for comfort · social work, counseling, and spiritual support · short-term inpatient care and respite stays to give caregivers a break.

What it generally doesn't

Room and board in a nursing home or facility (outside covered short-term stays), and treatment intended to cure the terminal illness after choosing hospice — and you can leave hospice at any time to pursue treatment again.

Medi-Cal

California's Medi-Cal offers a hospice benefit that mirrors Medicare's for eligible Californians — including many families who don't qualify for Medicare. If a loved one has both, they work together.

Every situation is a little different — Medicare Advantage plans, nursing-home residents, longer prognoses. Call us and we'll walk through what applies to your family before care begins, at no cost.
