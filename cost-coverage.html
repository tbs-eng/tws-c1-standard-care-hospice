Cost & Coverage

Does Medicare cover hospice care?

Yes — hospice care is typically covered by Medicare, Medi-Cal, and most private insurance plans, with no co-pays or deductibles for services related to the hospice diagnosis. For most families, hospice comes at no direct cost.

What's included at no cost to your family

Your care team

Nursing visits, aide support, social worker, chaplain, and volunteer support — coordinated with your own doctor.

Medications

Medications related to the hospice diagnosis — symptom relief and comfort.

Equipment & supplies

Hospital bed, oxygen, wheelchair, and the supplies care requires, delivered to the home.

Coverage, plainly

Medicare

The Medicare hospice benefit covers hospice care for eligible patients — certified by a physician, focused on comfort. Most families pay nothing out of pocket for covered services.

Medi-Cal

Medi-Cal provides hospice coverage for eligible Californians, mirroring the Medicare benefit for those who qualify.

Private insurance

Most plans include a hospice benefit — we help you check yours before care begins.

If none of these apply

Call us anyway. We'll talk through your situation honestly and point you the right way.

Questions families ask about cost

Are there any out-of-pocket costs?

There are no co-pays or deductibles for services related to the hospice diagnosis.

Will we get a bill?

For covered hospice services, families typically receive no bill. Before care begins, we walk through your specific coverage so there are no surprises.

Does choosing hospice mean losing our doctor?

No — your doctor stays involved, and we coordinate closely with them.

How do we find out what applies to us?
